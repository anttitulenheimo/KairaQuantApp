Standard orders fill completely at the decision price. Partial orders fill half first, then the remainder at the next synthetic hourly open. A price change can cause rejection if cash is insufficient.

The failure test cycles through five cases: a lost acceptance acknowledgement, partial fill, rejection, delayed response and canceled remainder. A lost acknowledgement deliberately pauses the run. Use Next step or reload and continue with the same order ID.

A rejected or canceled remainder is not resubmitted in the same month. Reopening the browser does not place a trade. The execution journal and simulated broker state are stored separately; inconsistent storage blocks further execution.

Prices and the broker are simulated. This tests software behavior, not strategy profitability or real broker execution quality. V8/V10 validation and the legacy paper application remain separate. This page sends no requests to a broker.
