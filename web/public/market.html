TECHNICAL MONITORING

Fresh data.
Simulated execution.

Both assets must have quotes no older than 60 seconds, an uncrossed spread of at most 2%, and two contiguous completed hourly bars. Hourly bars have a 60-second finalization delay. Missing data, regressing timestamps or revised observed bars block decisions.

The initial allocation uses the first valid batch. Later decisions occur at the UTC month boundary, with a maximum five-minute execution window. A restart never fills an old monthly order using a current price. Pausing stops data requests and simulated execution.

Quotes are polled at most once per 30 seconds, with backoff after failures. Balances, market evidence and simulated fills commit atomically. The service runs on your computer and requires its Python process to stay open.

This is technical shadow monitoring, not an independent profitability study. There is no broker order API, liquidity guarantee or slippage model. V8/V10 evaluations, frozen rules and data budgets remain separate. Offline fixture mode is always labeled explicitly.
